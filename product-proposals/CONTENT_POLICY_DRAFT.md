# Content policy and disclaimer (draft)

Status: draft for the owner's review, 2026-11-09. Not published and not in
force. It becomes live only when a reviewed version is added under `content/`
and linked from the sign-up and model pages. Have a lawyer read it before the
uncensored model is marketed; this draft is not legal advice.

## The short version shown to users

> AI Power Grid serves open models, including models with no built-in content
> filter. You must be 18 or older. You are responsible for what you ask for and
> what you do with the output. Content that is illegal is not allowed, and
> sexual content involving minors is banned without exception. Your prompts
> are processed by independent community operators who can read them; do not
> send secrets or personal data.

## Rules

1. **Adults only.** Users must be at least 18, or the age of majority where
   they live if that is higher.
2. **Legal content is allowed**, including mature and explicit fiction and
   roleplay between adult characters.
3. **Never allowed, on any model:**
   - Sexual content involving minors, or anyone presented as a minor. Reports
     are made to the relevant authorities where the law requires it.
   - Content that is illegal to create or possess where the user or the
     serving operator is located.
   - Sexual or intimate images of real people without their consent.
   - Use of the service to plan or carry out violence, harassment, fraud or
     the compromise of systems the user does not own.
4. **The user is responsible** for their prompts, for the output, and for
   complying with the law where they are. Output is generated by a model and
   may be wrong, offensive or unsafe; it is not professional advice.
5. **Privacy.** Requests run on hardware operated by independent community
   members, who can read prompts and outputs. The Grid does not offer a
   confidential tier today.
6. **Enforcement.** Accounts that break these rules can be suspended and their
   credit forfeited where the law allows. Operators may decline to serve
   uncensored models.

## Open questions for the owner

- Whether operators can opt out of serving uncensored models per worker. The
  rule above assumes they can; that is not built today.
- Whether image and video models follow the same rules. This draft assumes yes,
  with rule 3 applying to generated images of real people.
- Where reports go (an abuse address is needed before publishing).
- No automated filter enforces rule 3 today. State that honestly or add one.
